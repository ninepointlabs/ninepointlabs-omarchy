workspace 6 · who's building this

    
Nine Point Labs

    
I'm Tim: combat veteran, semi-retired technologist, full-time dad in Whitehouse, Texas. Omarchy runs on every machine I sit down at, and these projects are my way of giving something back to it.

    
I write some of the code and direct a small team of AI agents for the rest. My one real skill is seeing clearly what a tool should feel like, and being stubborn until it does.

    
Why nine? In the Army, a nine-line is a short report with nine items. You fill it in, send it, and help is on the way. No fluff, nothing extra, and it works when it counts. I carried that idea with me from the infantry into building software. Nine Point Labs makes small, clear tools for Linux and the open web that do their job and stay out of your way. (And yes, the 'Labs' is a little nod to Bell Labs.)
